# Randomized funny announcer intros

## Goal
Instead of one welcome line, give The Elf 10 different over-the-top intros. A random one plays each time the host opens the lobby — so it feels fresh every session.

## What changes

### 1. `src/lib/announcer.functions.ts`
Replace the single `vo_welcome` entry with 10 numbered intro slots: `vo_welcome_1` … `vo_welcome_10`. Each gets generated by ElevenLabs the next time the admin clicks **Generate AI announcer pack**.

Proposed lines (loud, goofy, Elf-energy):

1. "Welcoooome to BEAT! THE! DROP! The trivia showdown where brains get BROKEN and egos get BRUISED!"
2. "Ohhh we got a LIVE one tonight! Strap in nerds — it's BEAT THE DROP, baby!"
3. "Ladies, gentlemen, and whatever Greg identifies as today — welcome to BEAT THE DROP!"
4. "Welcome to BEAT THE DROP, where your relationship will not survive round three!"
5. "It's the show your therapist warned you about — BEAT! THE! DROOOOP!"
6. "Welcome contestants! Or as I like to call you... future losers! Let's play BEAT THE DROP!"
7. "Buckle up buttercups, it's BEAT THE DROP — the only game where being smart is a personality flaw!"
8. "Welcome to BEAT THE DROP! Tonight one of you becomes a legend. The rest of you become CONTENT."
9. "Heyyyy gamers! Put down the snacks, pick up the buzzers — it's BEAT THE DROP TIME!"
10. "Welcome to BEAT THE DROP! Where trivia goes to DIE and friendships go with it!"

### 2. `src/lib/sound-engine.ts` (or wherever the lobby opens)
Add a tiny helper that, when the host hits the lobby, picks a random `vo_welcome_*` clip from the `Announcer` folder and plays it once.

### 3. Soundboard UI
The 10 clips show up as individual buttons in Admin → Soundboard under the **Announcer** folder so you can preview each one and re-roll any that flop.

## After shipping
Click **Generate AI announcer pack** once — it'll burn ~10 extra TTS calls but you'll have a permanent rotation. Open the host lobby a few times to hear different ones.
